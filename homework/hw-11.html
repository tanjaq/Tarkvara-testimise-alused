Homework 11 — Accessibility Audit

Individual or pairLesson 12Submit: Teams

Objective

Find real accessibility defects with a keyboard, your eyes and one scanner — and see how much the scanner misses.

Task

  1. Pick one public site (a shop, a bank, a public service, a news site) and your own project.
  2. Keyboard-only walkthrough. Put the mouse away. Complete one real task using only Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Space and the arrow keys. Record: anything unreachable, any point where the focus indicator disappeared, any keyboard trap, any order that made no sense.
  3. Zoom. Take the page to 200% and then 400%. Does content reflow, or is text cut off and overlapping?
  4. Contrast. Check body text, placeholder text, buttons and disabled states. Normal text needs at least 4.5:1, large text and UI components 3:1.
  5. Colour only. Find at least one place where information is conveyed by colour alone — or confirm there is none.
  6. Structure. Check headings (one h1, no skipped levels), image alt text, form labels (a placeholder is not a label), link text out of context, and error messages.
  7. Automated scan. Run axe DevTools, WAVE or Lighthouse on the same pages.
  8. Optional but recommended: turn on VoiceOver, NVDA or TalkBack and try one flow with your eyes closed.
  9. Report. One row per finding: page, what is wrong, the WCAG success criterion it breaks (e.g. 1.4.3 Contrast, 2.1.1 Keyboard, 2.4.7 Focus Visible, 3.3.2 Labels), severity, and how you would fix it.
  10. Finish with a comparison: how many findings came from the automated scan, and how many only from your manual checks?

Deliverable

An audit report for both sites with findings mapped to WCAG criteria, plus the manual-versus-automated comparison and a fix for at least one finding in your own project.

Assessment criteria

CriterionWhat we look for
Manual workThe keyboard, zoom, contrast and structure checks were actually done, not just a scan
WCAG mappingFindings reference specific success criteria
SeverityAssigned by user impact, and justified
ComparisonYou can say what the scanner missed
Your own projectAt least one accessibility defect in your own project is found and fixed
