Homework 2 — Test Process Worksheet — Calculator

Group of 3–4Lesson 3Submit: Teams

Objective

Experience the full test process end to end on something small enough to finish in one session.

Task

  1. The system under test: a simple web calculator that takes two numbers, offers four operations (add, subtract, multiply, divide) and shows a result.
  2. 1. Planning and control — define the scope of testing. What are the risks? (division by zero, negative numbers, empty fields, very large numbers, decimal separators)
  3. 2. Analysis and design — identify test conditions. Choose which conditions matter most, based on risk, frequency and criticality.
  4. 3. Test creation — write at least three test cases with inputs, steps and expected result.
  5. 4. Test execution — assume the calculator is built (you may use your OS or phone calculator). Mark each test PASS or FAIL. For a FAIL, write a short defect description.
  6. 5. Closure — was the testing sufficient? What was left uncovered? What would you improve next round?
  7. Filling it in — either download the Word version and type into it, or type straight into the worksheet page and use its Print button to save a PDF. Hand the finished file in through Teams.
  8. Present to the class and discuss whether the testing was enough.

Deliverable

The completed worksheet with all five sections filled in.

Assessment criteria

CriterionWhat we look for
CompletenessAll five process steps are filled in, not just the test cases
Risk thinkingRisks named are real and specific to a calculator
Test casesAt least three cases with concrete inputs and a single, measurable expected result
Honest closureYou can name what you did not cover
