Homework 12 — Mobile Test Charter

Individual or pairLesson 13Submit: Teams

Objective

Test the conditions that only exist once the software is in someone's hand.

Task

  1. Part 1 — device matrix. For your own project (or a chosen app), decide which devices you would test on. Give five rows: device or class, OS version, screen size, why it is on the list, and what you would prioritise testing there. Include one deliberately old, small and slow device.
  2. Say where each row would be tested: real device, emulator, or device cloud — and why.
  3. Part 2 — the session. Write a charter: Explore <app> under interruption and poor network to discover state-loss defects.
  4. Time-box it to 45 minutes on a real phone. Start a real flow — a search, a booking, a multi-step form, a checkout.
  5. During the flow, apply the conditions from the lesson: rotate the device · switch to another app and come back · turn on airplane mode mid-request · switch WiFi to mobile data · let the screen lock · deny a permission and retry · tap the submit button twice quickly · use a very large system font.
  6. Record what happens each time: state lost, duplicate submission, frozen screen, silent failure, unreadable layout, unreachable button.
  7. Part 3 — report. Write up your findings as defects with severity, and pick the single worst one to write as a full bug report.
  8. Note which conditions found the most defects — that is your priority list for next time.

Deliverable

A five-row device matrix with justification, your session notes, the defect list with severities, and one full bug report.

Assessment criteria

CriterionWhat we look for
Matrix reasoningDevices are chosen from real usage considerations, not at random
Real deviceThe session was run on an actual phone, not only an emulator
Condition coverageInterruption, network, orientation and permission conditions were all tried
DefectsReal findings with enough detail to reproduce
PrioritisationYou can say which conditions were most productive and why
