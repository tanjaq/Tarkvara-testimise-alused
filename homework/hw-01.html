Homework 1 — The Quality Mindset

Individual · discussionLesson 1Submit: No formal submission — discussed in class

Objective

Start thinking about quality as something you can describe in specific terms, rather than a feeling.

Task

  1. Choose one piece of software you consider high quality and one you consider poor quality. Everyday apps are fine — a bank app, a ticket site, a game.
  2. For each, name which of the seven quality aspects it delivers on or fails: understandable design, suitable functionality, reliability, consistency, durability, after-sales service, value for money.
  3. Write 3–5 sentences on what "quality" means to you after this lesson.
  4. Be ready to present in 2 minutes.

Deliverable

Nothing to hand in. Come prepared to speak.

Assessment criteria

CriterionWhat we look for
SpecificityYou name concrete behaviour, not "it feels slow"
VocabularyYou use the quality aspects from the lesson
ReflectionYour definition of quality goes beyond "no bugs"
