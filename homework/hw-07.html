Homework 7 — Decision Table — Photo Ordering

Individual or pairLesson 7Submit: Teams — decision table

Objective

Apply a specification-based technique to a rule set complex enough that intuition fails.

Task

  1. A user wants to print holiday photos. The photo ordering website has the following business rules:
  2. • An unregistered regular user has no permanent discount.
    • A registered user gets a 5% discount on every purchase.
    • Additional discounts: ordering more than 10 photos adds 3%; ordering photo frames as well adds 3%.
    • On a registered user's birthday, the standard 5% is replaced by 8%.
  3. Build the decision table showing every possible combination of the business rules and the discount that applies to the user.
  4. Mark which combinations you would test first, and explain why (risk, likelihood, business impact).
  5. Note any rule that is ambiguous as written — that is a defect in the requirement, and finding it is part of the exercise.

Deliverable

The complete decision table plus your prioritisation and any ambiguities found.

Assessment criteria

CriterionWhat we look for
CompletenessEvery combination of conditions appears
Correct outcomesThe discount for each row is calculated correctly
PrioritisationYou can justify which rows to test first
Requirement reviewAmbiguities in the rules are identified
